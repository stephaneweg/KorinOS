# Morpho

An egg has just appeared. A small, unique creature will hatch from it and grow up with you: **baby**,
**child**, **teenager**, **adult**, then **elder**. At each stage it changes: legs, horns, ears, mouth, back, wings and
colours are drawn at random, from what it was and the care it received. A well cared-for creature often has brighter
colours, and sometimes wings.

Time really passes, even while Morpho is closed (a little more gently).

## Its needs

The gauges, on the left:

| Gauge | What fills it |
|---|---|
| **Fullness** | Feeding: a meal (a lot), a fruit (a little, and good for health), a treat (mostly happiness) |
| **Happiness** | Playing ball, cuddling, a treat |
| **Energy** | Sleep: it falls asleep when tired or at night; switch the light off and it rests twice as well |
| **Cleanliness** | Washing; picking up its droppings (click them) |
| **Health** | Drops when it is hungry, dirty or eats too many treats; when ill, it needs two doses of medicine |

When it needs something, a bubble appears above its head.

## Actions

- **Feed** (N), then **Meal**, **Fruit** or **Treat** (1, 2, 3).
- **Play** (J): a ball appears; click in the scene to throw it, it runs to catch it and brings it back.
- **Wash** (L): a bubble bath, and everything cleaned up.
- **Heal** (O): medicine (it does not like it, but it cures it).
- **Bedtime / Light on** (E): switch the light off for the night, or back on.
- **Cuddle** (C), or drag the mouse over it: it closes its eyes with pleasure.
- **Card** (I): its scientific name, character, weight, age, when the next stage comes, the care received.

Dragging anywhere else, or the right button, turns the view; the wheel zooms in and out.

## Growing up, and after

Going from one stage to the next is a metamorphosis: it rises, spins and changes before your eyes. An elder finally
flies off to the stars, leaving an egg: the next generation keeps a little of its colours. Left too long without care,
it leaves earlier, and also leaves an egg.

## Pace

The **Pace** menu speeds time up (×10, ×60) to watch it grow without waiting for days. **Game > New egg** starts over;
**Rename** (F2) gives it another name.

The save file is `HOME:/morpho/pet.ini`.
