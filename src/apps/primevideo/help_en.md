# Prime Video

Watch Prime Video in Korin: **Prime Video's site** in its own window, with its icon, on Jet's engine. Everything the site
does is there.

Korin PC only: Prime Video protects its videos with **Widevine**, Google's module that Jet can run on the PC.

## The first time

1. **The Widevine module**: if it is not there yet, Prime Video offers to download it from Google (about 22 MB, checksum
   verified) and to install it in your home folder. It is the same one as Jet's (Settings, "Play protected
   content") and Spotify's: one download for all.
2. **Your account**: sign in in the window, as on the site, then choose your profile. The app keeps its own session
   (its cookies are its own, apart from Jet's): next time, nothing to do again.

## Watching

The window opens maximized. **F11** (or **File > Agrandir**) maximizes it or brings it back to its size.

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| Space | play or pause |
| F11 | maximize the window, or restore it |
| Ctrl+R, F5 | reload the page |
| Alt+← | previous page |
| Ctrl+W, Ctrl+Q | close |

## The File menu

- **Reload**, **Back**: the page.
- **Agrandir** (maximize): like F11.
- **Open in Jet**: Prime Video in the browser (with Jet's session).
- **Sign out (clear the data)…**: clears the app's session (cookies, storage); Jet's does not change.

## Good to know

- The Widevine module runs here as in Chrome on Linux. Prime Video limits the picture to **HD 720p** or less, as for any browser on Linux.
- How smooth the video is depends on Jet's drawing, still being improved on the PC.
