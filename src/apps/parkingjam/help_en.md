# Parking Jam

Empty the parking lot: every car must drive out onto the road without being blocked by another.

## Playing

- **Tap or click a car**: it drives along its axis, towards the side you touched, until it hits a car, a cone or the
  edge, or leaves onto the road.
- **Swipe** on a car to choose the direction yourself.
- The level is won when the lot is empty: **Next level**, or **Replay this level** to do better.

At the top, the arrows move between the levels already unlocked; **Undo** takes back the last move, **Restart**
starts the level again. Your progress is kept for next time.

## Good to know

Parking Jam is made with Godot. It needs the **godot-templates** package (Software), which contains what Godot games
need to run. Its project ships with Korin's sources, so you can open and change it in Godot.
