# Relics

An explorer sets out to find twenty-one relics hidden across seven worlds: the Ixtala Jungle, the Sands of Kharga, the Pyramid of Sekhet, the Ice Fortress, the Wood of Shadows, Death Valley and Doctor Krell's Volcano. Each level scrolls as you move; reach the exit door while dodging traps and guards. A single touch costs a life: watch first, then move at the right moment.

## Keyboard

- **Left** / **Right**: walk.
- **Up** / **Down**: climb up or down a ladder.
- **Space** (or **Up** away from a ladder): jump. Hold the key to jump higher.
- **Down** + **Space**: drop through a plank.
- **X**, **Ctrl** or **F**: fire the pistol (one bullet takes down an enemy).
- **D**, **C** or **Down** + fire: drop a stick of dynamite. It goes off after two seconds: get away!
- **P**, **Esc** or the **‖** button at the top right: pause menu (Resume, Restart level, Quit).
- **S**: sound on or off.

On the title screen, **Left** and **Right** pick a level you have already reached; **Space** or **Enter** starts the game.

## Gamepad

The d-pad to walk and climb, **A** to jump, **B** or **X** to fire, **Y** for dynamite, **Start** for the pause menu.

## Traps

- **Spikes** on the floor or the ceiling, **water**, **quicksand**, **slime** and **lava**: deadly.
- **Hidden spikes**: thin slots in the floor. They shoot up a moment after you step on them: don't stop there.
- **Darts**: a carved head in the wall fires when you are in its line. Jump over them.
- **Boulders**: two tiles high, they start rolling towards you. In a big hall, jump over them; in a low corridor you can only run: keep going and jump the pit at the end, the boulder will fall into it.
- **Stalactites**: they shake, then fall when you walk under them.
- **Crushers**: spiked blocks that slam down at regular intervals; go through while they rise.
- **Fire jets**: a wisp of smoke warns of them.
- **Fragile floors**: they collapse shortly after you step on them, and come back later.

## Items

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Coin, gem, vase | 200, 400 or 300 points |
| Relic (golden statuette) | 2,000 points |
| Box of bullets | 6 bullets |
| Dynamite | 3 sticks |
| Key | opens a door (walk into it) |
| Heart | one life |
| Torch on a post | checkpoint: you start again here after a fall |

Cracked walls give way to dynamite. At the end of a level, each bullet left is worth 50 points, each stick 100, and collecting every treasure 3,000. An extra life every 15,000 points. Each level starts with at least six bullets and six sticks.

The level reached and the best score are kept in your home folder (`HOME:/relics/progress.txt`).
