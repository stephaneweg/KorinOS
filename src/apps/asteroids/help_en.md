# Asteroids

Fly a ship through an asteroid field. Each large asteroid you hit splits into two medium ones, each medium one into two small ones; a small one is destroyed. Flying saucers show up now and then and shoot at you. The edges of the screen wrap around: whatever leaves one side comes back on the other.

## Keyboard

- **Left** / **Right**: rotate the ship.
- **Up**: thrust (the ship keeps its momentum in space).
- **Space**: fire (hold to keep firing).
- **Down** or **H**: hyperspace jump (you reappear at random, sometimes in the wrong place).

## Mouse

- **Move the mouse**: the ship turns towards the pointer and flies to it.
- **Left click**: fire (hold for a burst).
- **Right click**: hyperspace.

## Everything else

- **P**, **Esc** or the **‖** button at the top right: pause menu (Resume, Restart, Quit).
- **S**: sound off or on. **F2**: new game. **F11**: enlarge the window.

## Scoring

| Target | Points |
|---|---|
| Large asteroid | 20 |
| Medium asteroid | 50 |
| Small asteroid | 100 |
| Large saucer | 200 |
| Small saucer | 1000 |

An extra life every 10,000 points. The high score is kept in your home folder (`HOME:/asteroids/best.txt`).
