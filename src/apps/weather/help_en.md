# Weather

The current weather and the forecast for the next four days, for a city.

## Choosing a city

1. Type the city name in the first field ("Liège", "Lyon"…).
2. If needed, its country in the second one: the name or the two-letter code (BE, FR, CH…). Useful when several
   cities share the same name.
3. **Enter**, or the **Search** button.

The card shows the place found, its local time, the temperature, how it feels, the humidity, the wind, then the next
four days (the maximum in bold, the minimum next to it). The last city is kept for the next time.

## Refreshing

The forecast refreshes by itself every 15 minutes. **File > Refresh** (Ctrl+R) reloads it at once; **File > Change
city** (Ctrl+L) puts the cursor in the city field.

## The card on the desktop

**File > Show on the desktop** puts the same card on the desktop, below the Agenda, behind the windows. It comes back
at every session (it is added to your startup programs: Settings, Startup shows it). A click on the card opens
Weather, to change the city: the card follows. Untick **Show on the desktop** to remove it.

## The network

Weather needs the Internet. Without a network, the card says so and tries again every minute.
